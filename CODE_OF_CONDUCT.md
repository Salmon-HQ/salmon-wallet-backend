# Salmon Wallet Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Salmon is building open, community-owned wallet infrastructure. We want participation in our project and community to be welcoming, safe, and constructive for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

This Code of Conduct applies across Salmon community spaces, including GitHub, Telegram, social channels, online conversations, and events where someone represents Salmon.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, kind, and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give feedback about ideas and work, not personal characteristics.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information without permission.
- Keep conversations relevant to the space and helpful to the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or discrimination.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or other NSFW content.
- Illegal content or activity.
- Sharing private information without explicit permission.
- Spam, phishing, scams, malicious links, or deceptive activity.
- Impersonating Salmon team members, moderators, or other community members.

## Telegram community rules

In addition to the standards above:

- English is the community language.
- Do not promote other groups, channels, products, or services without admin approval.
- Avoid “text bombing” or sending many small messages in a row.
- Spam, scams, phishing links, and unauthorized advertising may result in an immediate ban.

To report a Telegram message or user, reply to the relevant message with `@admin` or `/report`.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns outside Telegram, contact [hello@salmonwallet.io](mailto:hello@salmonwallet.io). Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as privately as reasonably possible.

Do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers and moderators may remove content, reject contributions, issue a warning, restrict participation, or temporarily or permanently ban someone whose behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

The response will depend on the severity, impact, and history of the behavior. Scams, credible threats, serious harassment, or attempts to compromise community members may result in an immediate permanent ban.
